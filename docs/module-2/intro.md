# Module 2: The Digital Twin (Gazebo & Unity)

## Introduction

Before deploying a humanoid robot in the real world, you need to test it somewhere safe. **Digital twins** — virtual replicas of physical robots — allow you to simulate, test, and refine robot behavior without risking expensive hardware.

This module covers the two most powerful simulation environments in robotics: **Gazebo** for physics-accurate simulation and **Unity** for photorealistic rendering.

## Why Simulation Matters

### The Cost of Real-World Testing

| Scenario | Without Simulation | With Simulation |
|----------|--------------------|-----------------|
| Fall during balance testing | Robot damage ($$$) | Reset simulation |
| Collide with obstacle | Hardware repair | Instant restart |
| Test 1000 gait variations | Months of trials | Hours of simulation |
| Train neural network | Dangerous + slow | Safe + fast |
