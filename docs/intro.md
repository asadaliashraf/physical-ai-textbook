# Welcome to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics 🤖

Welcome to the future of artificial intelligence—where digital minds meet physical bodies.

## Course Overview

The future of work will be a partnership between people, intelligent agents (AI software), and robots. This shift won't necessarily eliminate jobs but will change what humans do, leading to a massive demand for new skills.

This comprehensive textbook will guide you through the fascinating world of **Physical AI**—AI systems that function in reality and comprehend physical laws.

## What is Physical AI?

Physical AI represents a significant transition from AI models confined to digital environments to **embodied intelligence** that operates in physical space. It's the convergence of:

- 🧠 **Artificial Intelligence**: Deep learning, computer vision, natural language processing
- 🤖 **Robotics**: Mechanical systems, sensors, actuators
- 🌐 **Simulation**: Digital twins for training and testing
- 👁️ **Perception**: Understanding the physical world through sensors

## Why Humanoid Robots?

Humanoid robots are poised to excel in our human-centered world because they:

1. **Share our physical form**: They can navigate spaces designed for humans
2. **Can be trained with human data**: Abundant data from human interactions and demonstrations
3. **Natural interaction**: Familiar form factor makes human-robot collaboration intuitive
4. **Versatility**: Can perform tasks in environments built for humans without modification

## Course Structure

This course is organized into 4 comprehensive modules spanning 13 weeks:

### 📡 Module 1: The Robotic Nervous System (ROS 2)
**Weeks 1-3**

Learn to build the communication infrastructure that connects all parts of a robot:
- ROS 2 fundamentals: nodes, topics, services, and actions
- Python integration with rclpy
- Robot description formats (URDF)
- Package development and launch files

### 🌐 Module 2: The Digital Twin (Gazebo & Unity)
**Weeks 4-7**

Master physics simulation and create photorealistic virtual environments:
- Gazebo for physics-accurate simulation
- Unity for high-fidelity rendering
- Sensor simulation (LIDAR, cameras, IMUs)
- Building custom robot models and worlds

### 🧠 Module 3: The AI-Robot Brain (NVIDIA Isaac™)
**Weeks 8-10**

Harness the power of AI-accelerated robotics:
- NVIDIA Isaac Sim for photorealistic simulation
- Isaac ROS for hardware-accelerated perception
- Visual SLAM and navigation (Nav2)
- Sim-to-real transfer techniques
- Reinforcement learning for robot control

### 👁️ Module 4: Vision-Language-Action (VLA)
**Weeks 11-13**

Bridge natural language and physical actions:
- Voice recognition and command processing
- LLM integration for cognitive planning
- Multi-modal interaction (speech, vision, gesture)
- **Capstone Project**: Build an autonomous humanoid

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

✅ **Understand** the principles of Physical AI and embodied intelligence
✅ **Master** ROS 2 for robotic system development
✅ **Create** sophisticated robot simulations in Gazebo and Unity
✅ **Develop** AI-powered perception and navigation systems
✅ **Design** humanoid robots capable of natural human interaction
✅ **Integrate** GPT models for conversational robotics
✅ **Build** a complete autonomous humanoid system from scratch

## Prerequisites

### Software Skills
- **Python Programming**: Intermediate level (functions, classes, modules)
- **Linux Basics**: Command line, file system, package management
- **Basic Math**: Linear algebra, calculus (helpful but not required)

### Hardware Requirements

This course is technically demanding. It sits at the intersection of three heavy computational loads:

1. **Physics Simulation** (Isaac Sim/Gazebo)
2. **Visual Perception** (SLAM/Computer Vision)
3. **Generative AI** (LLMs/VLA)

#### Minimum Requirements:
- **GPU**: NVIDIA RTX 4070 Ti (12GB VRAM) or higher
- **CPU**: Intel Core i7 (13th Gen+) or AMD Ryzen 9
- **RAM**: 32 GB (64 GB recommended)
- **OS**: Ubuntu 22.04 LTS
- **Storage**: 256 GB SSD (512 GB recommended)

#### For Physical AI Experiments:
- NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano (8GB) or Orin NX (16GB)
- Intel RealSense D435i or D455 camera
- USB Microphone/Speaker for voice interface

:::tip Cloud Alternative
If you don't have access to the recommended hardware, you can use cloud-based solutions like AWS RoboMaker or NVIDIA's Omniverse Cloud. See [Appendix B: Software Setup](/docs/appendices/software-setup) for details.
:::
